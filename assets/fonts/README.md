Self-hosted print fonts

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl

Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Noto Sans Mono and Caveat, distributed under the accompanying SIL Open Font Licenses. WOFF2 subsets retain Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended and Uzbek punctuation. The heavy face is an instance of Noto Sans (weight 900, width 62.5). Caveat falls back within its family to self-hosted Noto Sans for characters it lacks. Legacy Arial/Georgia/Impact/Courier New/Pacifico family aliases keep saved designs renderable; the picker uses the real family names.
